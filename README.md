# Olist Marketplace Performance & Customer Experience Analysis

> An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing marketplace performance, delivery operations, customer satisfaction, retention, and key business drivers using the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

---

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the **Data Analytics Hackathon conducted by Gradient**.

The objective of this analysis is to understand how the Olist marketplace performed over time and identify the factors most strongly associated with customer satisfaction and dissatisfaction.

The project transforms raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations across multiple dimensions, including:

- Marketplace growth
- Order and revenue trends
- Delivery performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Geographic patterns
- Seller performance
- Product category performance
- Customer retention
- Root cause analysis

---

# Business Problem

As an e-commerce marketplace grows, managing customers, sellers, products, payments, logistics, and deliveries becomes increasingly complex.

Olist's leadership needs an evidence-based understanding of:

- How the marketplace is performing over time
- Which factors are associated with customer satisfaction and dissatisfaction
- How delivery performance relates to review scores
- Where geographic and operational challenges exist
- Which customers and sellers contribute most to revenue
- What the business should prioritize to improve the overall customer experience

The goal is **not to claim causation from observational data**, but to identify strong patterns and relationships that can support better business decisions.

---

# Dataset

The analysis uses the **Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist**.

### Dataset Period

**September 2016 – October 2018**

### Dataset Size

Approximately **100,000 e-commerce orders**

### Data Includes

- Customers
- Orders
- Order Items
- Products
- Sellers
- Payments
- Customer Reviews
- Geolocation Information

The datasets were combined to create a consolidated analytical view of the marketplace.
